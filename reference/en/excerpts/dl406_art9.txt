Art 9 The tax base is the price
of the service.

§ 1 When it is a
matter of the provision of services in the form of the taxpayer's own personal
work, the tax shall be calculated, by means of fixed or variable rates, based
on the nature of the service or other pertinent factors, not including in these
the amount paid as remuneration for the work itself.

§ 2
In the execution of hydraulic works or civil construction, the tax shall be calculated
on the price minus the portions corresponding to:

a) the value of materials acquired from
third parties, when supplied by the service provider;

b) the value of subcontracts already
taxed by the tax.

§ 2 In the provision of the services referred to
in items 19 and 20 of the attached list, the tax shall be calculated on the price minus the
corresponding portions:     (Wording given by Decree-Law No. 834, of 1969)

a)
the value of materials supplied by the service provider;
(Wording given by Decree-Law No. 834, of 1969)

b)
the value of subcontracts already taxed by the tax.
(Wording given by Decree-Law No. 834, of 1969)

3. When the services referred to in items I, III, IV (only industrial
property agents), V and VII of the attached list are rendered by companies,
these shall be subject to the tax in the form of § 1, calculated in relation to each
qualified professional, partner, employee or not, who renders services in the name of the
company while assuming personal responsibility, under the terms of the applicable
law.

§
3 When the services referred to in items 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 11, 12 and 17 of the attached
list are rendered by companies, these shall be subject to the tax in the form of §
1, calculated in relation to each qualified professional, partner, employee or not, who
renders the service in the name of the company, while assuming personal responsibility, under
the terms of the applicable law.       (Wording given by Decree-Law No. 834, of 1969)

 § 3 When the services referred to in items 1, 4, 8, 25, 52,
88, 89, 90, 91 and 92 of the attached list are rendered by companies, these shall be subject
to the tax in the form of § 1, calculated in relation to each qualified professional,
partner, employee or not, who renders services in the name of the company, while assuming
personal responsibility, under the terms of the applicable law.      (Wording given by Complementary Law No. 56, of
1987)

§ 4
In the provision of the service referred to in item 101 of the Attached List, the tax is
calculated on the portion of the price corresponding to the direct proportion of the
length of the highway operated, within the territory of the Municipality, or of half the length of a
bridge that joins two Municipalities.     (Included by Complementary Law No.
100, of 1999)

§ 5 The tax
base determined under the terms of the preceding paragraph:     (Included by
Complementary Law No. 100, of 1999)

I - is reduced, in Municipalities where
there is no toll collection point, to sixty percent of its value;
(Included by Complementary
Law No. 100, of 1999)

II – is increased, in Municipalities
where there is a toll collection point, by the addition necessary for it to be complete
in relation to the highway operated.
(Included by Complementary
Law No. 100, of 1999)

§ 6 For purposes of
the provision of §§ 4 and 5, the highway
operated is considered to be the stretch bounded by the points equidistant between each toll collection
point, or between the nearest of them and the initial or terminal point of the highway.     (Included by
Complementary Law No. 100, of 1999)
