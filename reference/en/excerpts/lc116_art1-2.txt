        Art. 1. The Tax on Services of Any Nature, within the competence of the Municipalities and the Federal
   District, has as its taxable event the provision of services listed in the attached list, even if such services do not
   constitute the predominant activity of the provider.

         § 1. The tax also applies to services originating from abroad or whose provision has begun
   abroad.

         § 2. Except for the exceptions expressly stated in the attached list, the services mentioned therein are not
   subject to the Tax on Transactions Relating to the Circulation of Goods and on Interstate and
   Intermunicipal Transportation and Communication Services – ICMS, even if their provision involves the supply of
   goods.

        § 3. The tax provided for in this Complementary Law also applies to services rendered through the use
   of public goods and services economically exploited under authorization, permission or concession, with the
   payment of a tariff, price or toll by the final user of the service.

          § 4. The levy of the tax does not depend on the name given to the service rendered.

          Art. 2. The tax does not apply to:

          I – exports of services to abroad;

        II – the provision of services under an employment relationship, by casual workers, by directors and members of
   an advisory board or fiscal council of companies and foundations, as well as by managing partners and
   delegate managers;

         III – the amount intermediated in the securities market, the amount of bank deposits, the principal,
   interest and late-payment charges relating to credit transactions carried out by financial institutions.

         Sole paragraph. Services developed in Brazil whose result is verified here are not covered by item I,
   even if payment is made by a resident abroad.
