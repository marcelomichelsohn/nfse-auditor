          7.02 – Execution, by administration, general contracting or subcontracting, of civil, hydraulic or
   electrical construction works and other similar works, including soil testing, well drilling, excavation, drainage and irrigation,
   earthmoving, paving, concreting and the installation and assembly of products, parts and equipment (except the
   supply of goods produced by the service provider outside the location where the services are rendered, which is
   subject to ICMS).
