 Information on the purpose of the IT – Technical Bulletin
    In general, the Technical Bulletin has the purpose of:

            •   Disclosing guidance and improvements for the DF-e Use Authorization
                Services, which are used by Companies;

            •   Disclosing and keeping a record of the update of domain tables used by the
                Authorization Service, which does not necessarily mean the need for changes
                to Companies' Computer Systems;

            •   Disclosing and keeping a record of guidance on providing information in the
                DF-e layout, informing on field completion and other matters;

            •   Disclosing and keeping a record of notices and other communication needs
                with companies.


            •   Revoking Technical Bulletin RT 2024.001 - v. 1.00 - Published on 12/07/2024.

 01. Purpose
    This Technical Bulletin has the purpose of disclosing the publication of the Table of Tax
    Classification Code of IBS and CBS, as well as the CST Indicators Table and the
    Table of Presumed Credit Classification available on the NF-e National Portal, under the
    "Documentos" tab, "Diversos" option, and to inform that Technical Bulletin RT 2024.001 - v1.00 is
    revoked.

    This Technical Bulletin also has the purpose of revoking Technical Bulletin RT 2024.001 - v1.00 and
    disclosing the tables necessary for the correct classification and taxation of IBS and CBS:

         •    Table of Tax Classification Code of IBS and CBS (cClassTrib)

         •    CST Table

         •    Table of Presumed Credit Classification (cCredPres)

         •    Table of Standard Rates of IBS and CBS (2026 to 2028)

    The tables are available on the NF-e National Portal, under the "Documentos" tab,
    "Diversos" option, and also on the portals of each DFe at the Electronic Tax Documents Portal -
    SVRS.



 02. Table of Tax Classification Code of IBS and
     CBS
    Complementary Law No. 214 of 01/16/2025 (LC 214/2025), approved by the Chamber of Deputies and forwarded
    for approval by the Federal Senate, established in Section VIII – Transitional Provisions, Art. 61, the
    obligation for the States, the Federal District and the Municipalities to adapt the current
    authorizing systems for Electronic Tax Documents (DFe) for the use of a
    standardized layout, allowing taxpayers to report data relating to the Tax on
    Goods and Services (IBS), the Contribution on Goods and Services (CBS) and the Selective Tax (IS).

    In compliance with this mandate, Technical Notes related to the tax reform were published,
    in addition to various other provisions, which introduced Group UB: Information on the
    IBS / CBS and Selective Tax taxes. In this group, positioned at the item level in the NF-e and the NFS-e,
    the taxpayer must report the codes "CST-IBS/CBS" and "cClassTrib".

    Each pair of codes "CST-IBS/CBS" and "cClassTrib" is associated with a specific provision
    of Complementary Law No. 214/2025 and supplementary rules, making the taxpayer's information

 03. CST Table
 This table has the purpose of assisting developers in filling in the field groups
 of IBS/CBS according to the CST used. To this end, it presents indicators that define the requirement,
 permission or prohibition of completing the respective fields in the Electronic Tax Document
 (DFe), as specified in a Technical Note.

 Table fields:


        •     CST-IBS/CBS: Tax Situation Code applicable to IBS and CBS, as defined in
              Complementary Law No. 214/2025.

        •     CST-IBS/CBS Description: Explanatory text describing the corresponding tax situation
              for the CST-IBS/CBS code.

        •     ind_gIBSCBS: Indicates whether the standard IBS and CBS information group
              must be completed in the tax document.

        •     ind_gIBSCBSMono: Indicates whether the specific group for operations subject

 04. Table of Classification of Presumed Credit of
     IBS and CBS
 The Table of Classification of Presumed Credit of IBS and CBS defines codes applicable to the
 legal cases of presumed credit of IBS and CBS, as provided for in Complementary Law No.
 214, of 2025. Each code refers to a specific situation authorized by the legislation.

 Table fields:

      •       cCredPres: Code that identifies the legal case of presumed credit of IBS and CBS.

      •       Description: Explanatory text describing the situation or legal case corresponding to the
              code.

      •       LC 214/2025: Provision of Complementary Law No. 214/2025 that grounds the grant of the
              presumed credit.

      •       Apropria via NF? Indicates whether the appropriation of the presumed credit can be made directly on the
              tax document (NF-e or NFSe).

      •       Apropria via evento? Indicates whether the appropriation of the presumed credit must be made by means of a
              specific event in the system.

      •       indDeduzCredPres: Indicates whether the presumed credit will be deducted in the calculation of the tax value
              of the item.

      •       ind_gCBSCredPres: Indicates whether the CBS presumed credit group must be completed in the
              tax document.

      •       ind_gIBSCredPres: Indicates whether the IBS presumed credit group must be completed in the
              tax document.

      •       Alíquota CBS: guidance on the rate applicable to the CBS presumed credit.

05. Standard rates of IBS and CBS
The standard rates of IBS and CBS, in percentage terms, to be reported in the electronic
tax documents are those defined in specific legislation for the respective year:

  Year                             pIBSUF (%)               pIBSMun (%)                  pCBS (%)
  2026 (LC 214/2025)                  0.1                        0                        0.9
  2027 (LC 214/2025)                 0.05                      0.05                Awaiting Legislation*
  2028 (LC 214/2025)                 0.05                      0.05                Awaiting Legislation*
  2029 onward                Awaiting Legislation*      Awaiting Legislation*      Awaiting Legislation(

* Each federative entity must set its rates by its own law (art. 14 of LC 214/2025).
If it fails to do so, the reference rate applies, set by resolution of the Federal Senate (art. 18).


06. Online Tables

 With the purpose of facilitating the understanding and correct completion of the Electronic Tax
 Documents (DF-e), these tables are also available on the DF-e National Portals in an
 online, interactive format:

 CST and Tax Classification (cClassTrib) Tables:
    https://dfe-portal.svrs.rs.gov.br/DFE/TabelaClassificacaoTributaria


 Presumed Credit Table (cCredPres):
    https://dfe-portal.svrs.rs.gov.br/DFE/TabelaCreditoPresumido

 The use of these tables is essential to ensure proper tax compliance, the
 correct completion of the mandatory fields in the electronic tax documents, and compliance
 with the validation rules established in the technical documentation of each DFe.

 In addition, the information can also be accessed in JSON format, through the services
 available at: https://dfe-portal.svrs.rs.gov.br/CFF/Servicos (access requires a digital certificate).
