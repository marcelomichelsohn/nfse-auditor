# Round protocol — written 08/09/2026, before any round

Agreed in the group on 08/09 (see `round-0-by-hand/REQUEST.md`, 12:30). The same protocol governs every round; a change to it is a new dated section below, never an edit above.

1. **Who.** The Employee (fiscal assistant of the office) runs the rounds; the Manager (office owner) chose the clients and reviews; Marcelo is present, transcribes and builds. Roles: `README.md` § Roles.
2. **What is recorded.** Screen + voice, the Employee narrating what she does, the difficulties, the errors, as detailed as she can. Every recording is transcribed verbatim, identifying information replaced or removed, and the transcript is committed as `round-N-*/transcript.md`.
3. **Round 0 — by hand.** The closing (step 5 of the office's process: checking each note) of one client with no errors — Marcelo's own company, the Manager's choice — done as today, no system. Baseline. A second client with errors if one appears at this month's closing (after the confidentiality agreement). First record of the repo; see `round-0-by-hand/`.
4. **Round 1 — with v1.** The folder loaded in the office's Claude project, recorded; feedback spoken in the video. **Different examples from round 0, and why (08/09):** v1 reads only NFS-e XML in the national standard; the notes of Marcelo's company exist only as the municipality's fixed-width text export (that municipality does not send to the national portal), so round 1 runs on notes the office itself issued, which exist as national XML. `time.md` of each round says which examples were used; a time difference between rounds is therefore not a before/after of the same work. Before the round, `expected.md` is committed (which fixtures/notes, which rows we expect, where we expect her to trip). After: `transcript.md`, `time.md` (her total time, her words, dated, with what the experiment does not prove), `what-changed.md` (what in the five files changed because of the round).
5. **Round 2 — with v2 (bonus).** The same examples as round 1, one last time, final video. If it does not happen the README says so.
6. **Volume.** Two clients with a medium-low number of notes, so the Employee does not spend much time. "Não precisamos de quantidade neste momento."
7. **Order proof.** `expected.md` is committed before `transcript.md`; a re-run is a new folder (`round-Nb-*`), never an overwrite; `tools/prove_order.py` checks the order at every close and its output is committed in `ORDER-PROOF.md`.
8. **What a round measures.** The Manager's words (12:34): "reduzir seu tempo no processo de fechamento da tributação mantendo a qualidade da entrega". Time in her words, dated; beside every time figure, what the experiment does not prove (one client, one operator, rounds on different days).

## Amendment — 08/09/2026, evening (after round 0 was watched)
- **Round 1 is not a before/after of round 0.** Round 0 showed the Employee closing a Simples client at the level of totals and codes, without opening an individual note; the folder audits individual notes. Round 1 is therefore: **an outsider opens the folder, tries to use it alone, and says what stuck and whether the report makes sense to her.** What is measured is the door (what stuck) and the sense the report makes, not minutes. `round-1-*/time.md` records her time as context only.
- **Date.** The day and time the Employee chooses (Wednesday or Thursday, about 30 minutes inside the office's closing), or none this week. If no round 1 happens by Thursday night, this repository goes to the deadline with round 0, the fixtures and the cold audits, and the README says the outsider did not open the folder this week. Round 2 only if the office offers it.
- **Examples.** Round 1 runs on the office's own notes in the national XML (see point 4 above); Marcelo's company's notes exist only as the municipality's text export.
