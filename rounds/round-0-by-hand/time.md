# Round 0 — time, in the operator's words, and what it does not prove

- **Recording length:** 18 min 36 s (screen recording; the automatic transcript's last timestamp is 18:16). Includes her explanations to the camera and a stretch (2:24 → 10:38) with no transcribed speech in which she worked across four systems, including an import she describes as slow — her words at 10:38: "ele está travando assim, muito por conta de que eu estou gravando".
- **The Manager's figures for the whole 7-step process, given in writing on 07/09 (report, not measurement):** 20 to 30 minutes per CNPJ when everything is right; 60 minutes or more when there are errors.
- **What this round measures:** one client (the author's own company), the clean case, one operator, the closing of a month other than August (August had no notes in the fiscal software because the municipality had not sent them to the national portal). It is a demonstration with narration, not a timed run.

**What this does not prove.** It is not a measurement of the 20–30 minute class: the narration and the software freeze are inside the 18 minutes. It does not show the note-by-note check this folder performs: **confirmed in the screen recording, no individual note was opened** — lists of notes appear and are not entered. Round 1 is therefore a **new task** for the Employee, not the same task with a tool. A shorter or longer time in round 1 is therefore not a before/after of the same work: round 1 runs on different notes (the office's own, in national XML) and on a different task (the per-note audit). The rounds are compared on what the Employee trips on and on what the folder catches, not on minutes.
