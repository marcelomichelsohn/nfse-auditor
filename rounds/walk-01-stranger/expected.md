# Walk 1 — expected (written 11/09/2026 afternoon, BEFORE the run; committed before the witness opens anything)

**Why this walk exists, and why it is not a round.** The rounds belong to the accounting office: the same operator, her own clients' notes, inside her working routine. This is not one of them, and it is kept out of the round chain on purpose — it is a *walk*, the name the author's previous entry used for the same thing. Rounds 1 and 2 measure the folder with an expert operator: she brings the domain with her, and where the text is thin her knowledge covers for it. This walk removes the domain. The witness is Leo, the author's son — no accounting, no contact with this repository — and he runs **alone**, with no call and no question answered. What is being measured is the seventeen commands, not him.

**Who and how.** The instruction he received, verbatim, is `instruction.md`. Route: the README read on GitHub in a browser; the seventeen commands as written; Chat, not Cowork; Opus 4.8 at low effort (steps 11–14); one XML attached. **On the author's Claude account**, decided 11/09 at 16:47, because the witness has no account with projects of his own: so step 6 meets a project already named `nfse-auditor` and has to be given another name — the collision the step was written for, met here for the second time (round 2 met it first). The note is a **public fixture** of this repository (`fixtures/mutations/m1-codigo-servico/nfse.xml` — a copy of a clean note with one field changed, `cTribNac` 171901 → 010101), so the run is publishable with nothing masked and any reader can repeat it. The profile line of step 16 is handed to him ready, in the instruction. **Fifteen minutes, hard**, decided 11/09 at 16:52: where he is when the fifteen minutes end is the measure, and not finishing is a result this walk needs, not a failed run. He records his screen with his microphone on and speaks **only** when he does not understand something or is confused, saying the step number, the sentence as written and what he understood from it; the recording is the trail, and the silences in it are the parts that worked.

**Contamination, declared before the run.** He knows his father has been building something for a competition. He has not seen the folder, the repository, a report, or any part of the rounds, and he was told what it is in the instruction's first paragraph and nothing beyond it. **He is in training in the author's method** for building folders of this kind: so he is not naive about the idea that a set of written instructions is the product, even though he has never seen this one. That cuts both ways and is declared rather than argued — it may make him readier to follow a numbered step, and it may make him harsher on a step that is badly written.

**He is named.** Everyone else in this repository is a role, and § Roles of `../README.md` said real names appear nowhere; that line was amended on the afternoon of 11/09, when the author decided that this witness appears as *Leo, the author's son, in training in the method*. The relation is the declaration: a reader has to weigh a son, and the weighing only works if the relation is on the page. The instruction he received offers him the choice of appearing without his name.

## What we expect, written first

1. **The door.** He reaches a report by following the commands, without asking the author anything. Where we expect him to stop:
   - **step 3** — the same place round 2 stopped ("eu não sei se eu entendi direito"). The step was reworded this afternoon, after round 2 and before this run, to name the file as the folder shows it and to say how to open it. This run is the first test of the rewording.
   - **steps 8 and 9** — the difference between the project's *instructions* and the project's *context*. The two words mean nothing outside the app, and one is a paste while the other is an upload.
   - **steps 13 and 14** — finding "Opus 4.8" in the full list of models, and finding the effort control.
   - **step 15** — the XML has to be saved from GitHub before it can be attached; a browser may display it instead of downloading it (he runs on Windows, and the instruction gives him Ctrl+S for that case).
   - **step 16** — he has the line ready, so the only risk left is pasting it somewhere other than the message box.
2. **The report, if the fifteen minutes reach it.** On this fixture the audit must return `1-código` **FALHA** (the note's code 010101 against the profile's item 1719), `1-descrição` NÃO DÁ PARA DETERMINAR, and `2`, `3`, `4`, `5-aritmética` PASSA, `5-alíquota` and `6` NÃO SE APLICA, then the totals line, "Não lidos: nenhum" and the CSV block with its sentence above it. The row-by-row gabarito is `../../expected/m1-codigo-servico.md`. **We do not expect him to judge a single row**: the fiscal content is outside his scope and the instruction says so.
3. **What counts as the door working.** Pass: he gets a report on screen inside the fifteen minutes, by himself, following only what is written. Round 2's expert operator took about ten minutes to the same point, with three prompts from the author and with the domain in her head, so fifteen minutes for a person from outside it is a tight window on purpose: what is being bought with it is the place where he stops. Fail: he cannot reach a report without asking; or he reaches one by doing something the steps do not say — uploading the zip itself into the project, using Cowork, choosing another model — because then the door was opened by his improvisation, not by the text.
4. **What is recorded beyond pass/fail:** from the recording — the step he was on at each minute; every sentence he read aloud as not understood, and the word in it; where the fifteen minutes ended; and, if he reached it, the time from the link to the report on screen.

## What this walk will not prove
One person, one afternoon, one note, fifteen minutes, and the person is the author's son, in training in the author's own method. The account is the author's, not his, so nothing here tests signing up or finding the app for the first time. He was told which note to use and handed the profile line, so nothing here tests composing that line from a client's sheet — round 2 tests that. He is not the product's operator: a stranger reaching the report says the door is passable, not that the report is right or useful to an accounting office.

## Why this file is committed before he starts
The author decides **after** the run whether this walk is published. That decision is only worth something if the prediction is already in the history, unchangeable, before the run exists: `../../tools/prove_order.py` checks, on a fresh clone, that this file's first commit precedes the first commit of `transcript.md` (walks are checked the same way the controls are, outside the numbered chain of the office's rounds). Predicting after the fact would cost nothing and prove nothing.
